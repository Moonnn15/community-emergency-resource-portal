STAY INFORMED

Safety guidelines

Practical reminders for heavy rainfall and flooding.

🌧️

During heavy rain

Stay indoors where safe, monitor official alerts, avoid unnecessary travel and keep away from drains.

🌊

During a flood

Move to higher ground when advised. Never walk or drive through floodwater or touch fallen wires.

🎒

Emergency kit

Prepare water, non-perishable food, medicines, first aid, flashlight, batteries and important documents.
